## Overview

Deposits let you fund your Doss wallet from an external source. The system supports four payment methods: **Bank Transfer**, **Stripe** (debit/credit card), **PayPal**, and **CoinPayments** (cryptocurrency). The available methods for each currency are configured by the administrator.

Deposit fees (percentage and fixed) are configured per currency and per payment method in Fees & Limits settings.

## Starting a deposit

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Deposit">
    Navigate to **Deposit** from the main menu. Select the currency you want to deposit into and the payment method.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the amount">
    Enter the deposit amount. The fee breakdown is displayed live. Only currencies that have an active deposit fee configuration are shown.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review fees">
    The confirmation screen shows:
    - Amount you are depositing
    - Percentage fee and fixed fee
    - Total amount that will be processed (amount + fees)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete payment">
    Follow the steps for your chosen payment method (see tabs below).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Payment methods

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Bank Transfer">
    Bank deposits require you to transfer funds to one of the configured bank accounts and then submit proof of payment.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Select a bank">
        After selecting Bank as the payment method, the available banks for your chosen currency are listed with account name, account number, and bank name.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Make the transfer">
        Transfer the deposit amount from your bank to the displayed account. Keep a record of your transfer reference.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Submit the deposit form">
        Return to Doss and submit the deposit form. You can attach a payment receipt file.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Wait for approval">
        Bank deposits are set to `Pending` status until an administrator reviews and approves the payment. You will receive an email notification when the status changes.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    <Note>
    Bank deposits are not credited to your wallet automatically. An administrator must manually approve the deposit after verifying receipt of funds.
    </Note>
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Stripe (Card)">
    Pay by debit or credit card using Stripe's payment gateway.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Proceed to card payment">
        Select **Stripe** as the payment method and continue to the card entry form.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Enter card details">
        Enter your card number, expiry month, expiry year, and CVC. All fields are required.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Authorize the payment">
        The system creates a Stripe Payment Intent and confirms it. On success, a `charge_id` and `receipt_url` are stored against the transaction.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Wallet credited">
        On successful card charge, your wallet balance is incremented immediately. The deposit and transaction records are created with status `Success`.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    <Tip>
    Stripe receipts are stored on the transaction. You can access the Stripe-hosted receipt URL from the transaction details.
    </Tip>
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="PayPal">
    Fund your wallet using a PayPal account.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Select PayPal">
        Choose **PayPal** as the payment method and proceed to the PayPal payment page.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Log in to PayPal">
        You are redirected to a PayPal-hosted page. Log in to your PayPal account and authorize the payment.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Return to Doss">
        After authorization, PayPal redirects you back to Doss. The deposit is processed and your wallet is credited.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    <Note>
    The PayPal payment uses the currency code of your selected wallet. Ensure your PayPal account supports the chosen currency.
    </Note>
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="CoinPayments (Crypto)">
    Deposit cryptocurrency using the CoinPayments gateway.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Select CoinPayments">
        Choose **CoinPayments** as the payment method. The system fetches live exchange rates and the list of accepted coins from the CoinPayments API.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Select a coin">
        For fiat currency deposits, select a cryptocurrency to pay with. The equivalent crypto amount is calculated from the live rate. For crypto currency wallets, the coin is selected automatically.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Send the crypto">
        A transaction is created via the CoinPayments API. You receive a payment address and amount to send. The transaction is recorded as `Pending`.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Wait for IPN confirmation">
        CoinPayments sends an IPN (Instant Payment Notification) callback to `coinpayment/check` when your transaction receives the required confirmations. The system automatically updates the deposit and transaction status and credits your wallet.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    <Note>
    CoinPayments deposits remain `Pending` until the required number of blockchain confirmations is received. The confirmation count is set by CoinPayments per coin.
    </Note>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Deposit status tracking

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `Pending` | Deposit submitted but not yet confirmed (bank transfer awaiting admin approval, or crypto awaiting blockchain confirmations) |
| `Success` | Deposit confirmed and wallet credited |

All your deposits appear in the **Transactions** list filtered by type `Deposit`.

## Fee calculation

Fees are calculated at the time of deposit based on the `FeesLimit` record for the selected currency and payment method:

```
percentage_fee = amount × (charge_percentage ÷ 100)
total_fees     = percentage_fee + charge_fixed
credited_amount = total_amount - total_fees
```

The amount credited to your wallet is `total_amount - total_fees`, not the gross amount entered.
