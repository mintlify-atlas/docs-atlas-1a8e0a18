## What is Doss?

Doss is a full-featured digital wallet and payment platform built on Laravel. It enables financial institutions, fintech companies, and businesses to offer their users a complete suite of payment services:

- **Send & Receive Money** — Peer-to-peer transfers between registered users with real-time notifications
- **Multi-Currency Wallets** — Support for fiat and cryptocurrency wallets with live exchange rates
- **Deposits** — Accept funds via bank transfer, Stripe, PayPal, and CoinPayments (crypto)
- **Withdrawals** — Configurable payout settings with multiple payment method support
- **Merchant Payments** — QR code-based payments and merchant payment processing
- **Invoicing** — Create, send, and collect payment on invoices
- **Request Money** — Send payment requests with dispute resolution workflow
- **KYC Verification** — Built-in identity and address proof verification
